Pack Test

Required before your first assignment. KY state qualifies emergency firefighters with the Moderate pack test, with the option to complete the Arduous.

  • Moderate — 2 miles in 30 min, 25 lb pack, flat terrain
  • Arduous — 3 miles in 45 min, 45 lb pack, flat terrain

Required Footwear

  • Leather boots, 8" minimum height
  • Vibram sole
  • No steel toe
  • No synthetic materials

PLEASE BREAK THEM IN BEFORE YOUR FIRST FIRE.

Don't just wear them once or twice — wear them everywhere, all the time. You'll look like an idiot, but when the first fire rolls around and you've already got 50 hours in your boots and your feet aren't turning into one giant blister, you'll be glad.

What to Bring

  • Nomex shirt and pants (Issued)
  • Hard hat (Issued)
  • Headlamp — check batteries!
  • Leather gloves (Issued)
  • Eye and ear protection (Issued)
  • Water — 2 liters minimum (think 2 Nalgenes)
  • Lunch and snacks for a full day
  • Homework, a book, or some way to pass the time
  • A good attitude and the ability to hurry up and wait!

Where to Meet

This is the location of our vans. Our SOP is to meet here at 10:00, wheels rolling by 10:15 — but that can change, so keep an eye on the group chat. At the light when you turn left into the farm, you'll see a gate that will probably be locked. I have the key card to unlock it, so just pull off to the side and I'll let you in when I get there. If it's open, or if someone comes out and it opens, feel free to park by our van and get your gear ready to go!
