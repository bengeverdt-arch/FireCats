Pack Test

Required to qualify for wildland fire. Walk 3 miles in 45 minutes carrying a 45 lb pack on flat terrain. Must pass before your first assignment.

Required Footwear

  • Leather boots, 8" minimum height
  • Vibram sole
  • No steel toe
  • No synthetic materials

PLEASE BREAK THEM IN BEFORE YOUR FIRST FIRE.

Don't just wear them once or twice — wear them everywhere, all the time. You'll look like an idiot, but when the first fire rolls around and you've already got 50 hours in your boots and your feet aren't turning into one giant blister, you'll be glad.
